Privacy Policy

Intra-City Ride Sharing & Pooling Platform

Application: Lo Ride (com.chaturveda.saathi.customer)

Welcome to Lo Ride ("we," "our," or "us"). We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe, transparent, and enjoyable experience while using our intra-city ride-sharing platform.

This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, how we use and safeguard it, and your choices regarding your information when using the Lo Ride mobile application on Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information necessary to provide seamless intra-city ride-sharing, passenger matching, and secure communication:

  • Account & Contact Details: When you sign in, we collect your 10-digit mobile number to verify your identity via a 2-factor one-time password (OTP). We also collect your full name and metro city. We do not store or require any passwords.
  • Location Information (Precise and Approximate):
    Why we collect Location:
    • To determine your pickup and drop-off points.
    • To find nearby drivers or passengers travelling along the same route.
    • To calculate route distances, estimated arrival times (ETA), and shared fares.
    • To provide live driver tracking for passenger safety during an ongoing ride.
    Location is accessed while the app is active in the foreground or during an active ongoing ride. You can revoke location permission at any time in device settings.
  • Microphone & Audio Access (In-App VoIP Calls):

    Lo Ride includes an in-app VoIP voice calling feature so drivers and passengers can coordinate pickups without exchanging personal phone numbers.

    • Audio streams directly between phones using WebRTC peer-to-peer encryption.
    • We DO NOT record, monitor, store, or wiretap voice calls.
    • In-app calling is strictly enabled only for active accepted rides and is permanently disabled as soon as a ride ends or is cancelled.
  • Driver Verification Documents: For users registering as drivers, we collect Driving License (DL) details and Vehicle Registration Certificates (RC) to verify identity, driver eligibility, and passenger safety.
  • In-App Chat Messages: Pickup coordination messages sent between driver and rider during an active ride. Messages are automatically archived upon ride completion.
  • Device & Push Notification Tokens: Device tokens to send you instant alerts when a driver accepts your ride, when your ride starts, when a message arrives, or when you receive a call.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information for the following specific purposes:

  • Facilitating carpooling and bikepooling ride matching between verified users.
  • Calculating transparent shared ride fares based on distance and vehicle type.
  • Enabling real-time VoIP audio calls and messaging without revealing phone numbers.
  • Verifying driver documentation to uphold platform safety standards.
  • Providing customer support, resolving disputes, and improving app performance.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We partner with trusted third-party providers to operate key infrastructure:

  • 2Factor.in: Telecommunications gateway used for sending SMS OTP verification codes.
  • Google Maps Platform: For map rendering, reverse geocoding, and route calculation.
  • Expo & Firebase (Google Cloud): For delivering push notifications.

These service providers only have access to information necessary to perform their respective functions and are prohibited from using it for any other purpose.

4. Data Sharing & Privacy Safeguards

We do not sell, rent, or monetize your personal data to any advertisers or data brokers.

During an active ride, only essential information (first name, vehicle make/model/plate, average rating, and pickup/drop location) is displayed to the matched driver or passenger. Personal phone numbers remain masked behind our in-app calling gateway.

5. Security of Your Data

We implement robust administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data. All network communication between the app and our servers is encrypted using industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS/HTTPS). Driver verification records are stored in secure, encrypted cloud databases.

6. Account Deletion & User Rights (Google Play Policy Compliance)

Under Google Play's User Data policy, you have full control over your personal information:

  • Right to View & Update: You can edit your name, city, and profile details anytime in the app.
  • Right to Permanent Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your Lo Ride account and associated data.
How to Delete Your Account and Data:

To request complete deletion of your account, phone number, vehicle documents, and ride history:

  1. Send an email to support@loloride.app with the subject Account Deletion Request.
  2. Specify your registered 10-digit mobile number.
  3. Upon verification, your account, personal data, verification documents, and ride history will be permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children's Privacy

Lo Ride is intended strictly for individuals aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 18.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Effective Date". Continued use of the app signifies acceptance of any updates.
